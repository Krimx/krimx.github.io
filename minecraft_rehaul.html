Minecraft Rehaul

Info

Minecraft is an incredible sandbox, but its survival experience can leave you wanting more. Minecraft Rehaul expands on the game you already know by giving existing features new purpose, encouraging exploration and interaction with the world, and extending progression with new resources and crafting paths.

Features

New Features
  • Diakrete: a new material as strong as diamonds, but lighter than water!
  • Ghast Resin: a mystical adhesive!
  • More golden crops: a rare product from regular crops!
  • Biome specific variations to vanilla ores!
  • Sculk can now be used as an armor trim material!
  • New armor trim patterns!
  • Level up villagers and give them the relevant items to research new trades!
  • Hold right-click to charge a swing attack with the mace!
  • Twilight Prismarine: a new byproduct of the conduit.
  • Use Echo Shards in an anvil to remove the enchantment penalty!
  • Drop concrete powder into water to instantly convert it!
  • Wingweave: a phantom fabric.
  • Tide Shell: a hard, lightweight alloy.
Changes
  • Tridents are now included in the ocean archeology loot pool.
  • Trial chambers have been revamped to fit earlier in the game's progression.
  • Removed golden carrot villager trade.
  • Removed mending book villager trade.
  • Removed furnace functionality; kept the block for building.
  • Stair recipes now use 3 blocks.
  • Blast furnace is now made with 8 clay.
  • New looks for gui elements.
Exploration

More to come...

Extended Progression
  • Wingwoven Elytra: grants a small speedboost when worn.
  • Framed Elytra: grants one armor bar when worn.
  • Framed Wingwoven Elytra: grants the effects of the Wingwoven Elytra and the Framed Elytra.
  • Elytra Chestplate: a diakrete chestplate with a Framed Wingwoven Elytra attached.
